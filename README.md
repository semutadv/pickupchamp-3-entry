One pickup line, at most 200 characters, in the voice of a robot trying to
charm someone. This entry grows the joke out of being a machine: the robot
has postponed every update all day — the purest avoidance of commitment it
knows — and offers the one surrender it has: a full restart, for you.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) writes the line to `entry.txt` in the
output directory, clearing the directory's contents first and adding no
trailing newline.
